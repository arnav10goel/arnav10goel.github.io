Research

My research interests lie in reliable machine learning. Recent work spans domain-specific verification, multi-agent reliability, model post-training, and token-efficient reasoning in large language models.

Prior work covers distillation of large models into efficient deployable ones, attribution of model behaviour to pre-training corpora, alignment and safety for enterprise language models, temporal and long-horizon user modelling, and multilingual speech and vision-language systems.

About

I have had the privilege of working with Dr. Aditi Raghunathan at CMU, Dr. Amit Sharma at Microsoft Research India, Dr. Xiang Ren at USC’s INK Lab, Dr. Jackie Chi Kit Cheung at Mila and McGill, and Dr. Anubha Gupta and Dr. Rajiv Ratn Shah during my undergraduate degree at IIIT Delhi. Outside research I am a big-time history buff and love watching documentaries. I have recently developed a strong penchant for cooking, and swimming has always been my way of unwinding. Please feel free to reach out — I am always glad to talk about language models and research more broadly, and especially happy to advise students finding their way into the field.
