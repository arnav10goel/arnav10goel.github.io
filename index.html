I am a Master's student in Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon, where I am a research assistant with Daniel Fried and Sean Welleck, working on efficient reasoning in language models and agents.

I study how to make language models and agents reason efficiently and reliably — and how to tell when they are doing neither.

About

This summer I am an Applied Scientist Intern with AWS Agentic AI in Seattle, working on verification and context routing for multi-agent systems. Back at CMU, my research is on reinforcement learning for efficient reasoning in LLMs and agentic systems.

Before this I spent time at Microsoft Research India, USC's INK Lab, Mila and McGill, and IBM Research, and did my undergrad at IIIT Delhi — working with a lot of lovely people along the way. Much of that work was about attributing what a model generates back to what it was trained on, and about measuring the harms models cause when they get it wrong.

Outside research I am a big-time history buff, an enthusiastic if unreliable cook, and always up for a game of squash. Please reach out if you would like to chat — I am always happy to talk about language models and research in general, and especially happy to give advice to students trying to find their way into it.
