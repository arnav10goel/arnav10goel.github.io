Research

My research interests lie in reliable machine learning. Recent work spans domain-specific verification, multi-agent reliability, model post-training, and token-efficient reasoning in large language models.

Prior work covers distillation of large models into efficient deployable ones, attribution of model behaviour to pre-training corpora, alignment and safety for enterprise language models, temporal and long-horizon user modelling, and multilingual speech and vision-language systems.

About

I have worked with Dr. Aditi Raghunathan at CMU, Dr. Amit Sharma at Microsoft Research India, Dr. Xiang Ren at USC’s INK Lab and Dr. Jackie Chi Kit Cheung at Mila and McGill. At IIIT Delhi I did my undergraduate research with Dr. Anubha Gupta and Dr. Rajiv Ratn Shah. Away from research I read a lot of history and watch more documentaries than I should. I took up cooking recently, and I play squash and swim to unwind. If you want to talk about language models or research, write to me. I am happy to help students who are starting out in research.
